This guide covers moving THEO from Autheo Mainnet to Robinhood Chain. You need this step before selling or trading THEO earned from validator rewards, staking, or the Node Sale on [Uniswap](https://app.uniswap.org/), where THEO trades in the THEO/USDG and THEO/NVDA pools on Robinhood Chain. Uniswap on Robinhood Chain cannot see THEO that is still on Autheo Mainnet, so it has to arrive on Robinhood Chain first.

<Note>
If you already hold THEO on Robinhood Chain (for example, THEO you bought on Uniswap), you can skip this guide. To trade on MEXC instead, no bridging is needed: MEXC accepts THEO deposits on Autheo Mainnet directly.
</Note>

## Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| Wallet | An EVM-compatible wallet where your THEO is stored (e.g. MetaMask) |
| Gas (source) | THEO on Autheo Mainnet, to pay the source-chain gas fee |
| Gas (destination) | ETH on Robinhood Chain, to pay gas when you later trade on Uniswap |

## 1. Add Robinhood Chain and THEO to your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Robinhood Chain">
    If Robinhood Chain is not already in your wallet, add it as a network. Use these details, or add it through Uniswap or your wallet's network list:

    | Field | Value |
    |---|---|
    | Network name | Robinhood Chain |
    | Chain ID | 4663 |
    | RPC URL | `https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com` |
    | Currency symbol | ETH |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add THEO as a custom token">
    With **Robinhood Chain** selected, open **Manage tokens**, choose **Add a custom token**, and enter the THEO contract address:

    ```
    0x3dc751e0da01812e6d9143cc696e7f07ea94da48
    ```

    Click **Add token**.

    <Warning>
    Always verify this contract address against this page or [docs.autheo.com](https://docs.autheo.com) directly before adding it. Never trust a contract address sent to you in a message or DM.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm THEO is visible">
    THEO now appears in your wallet's token list for Robinhood Chain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Bridge THEO using the Autheo Bridge

The flow is the same as [bridging to Base](/web-apps/bridge/bridging-theo-to-base); only the destination network changes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Autheo Bridge">
    Navigate to [https://hyper.autheo.com/](https://hyper.autheo.com/) and click **Connect Wallet** to connect the wallet holding your THEO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Token Transfer tab">
    Click the **Token Transfer** tab. Keep the source network (**From**) as **Autheo Mainnet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the destination to Robinhood">
    The destination (**To**) defaults to **Base Mainnet**. Click it and select **Robinhood** (Robinhood Chain).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount and recipient">
    Enter the amount of THEO to bridge. Enter the recipient address, or click **Self** if you are bridging to the same wallet, then click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Double-check the recipient and remote token">
    Verify the recipient address is correct. Then verify the **Remote Token** address matches `0x3dc751e0da01812e6d9143cc696e7f07ea94da48` before you send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the transaction">
    Confirm the transaction request in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bridging complete">
    Once the bridge completes, your THEO is on Robinhood Chain and ready to trade on Uniswap. Most transfers complete within a few minutes. You can follow progress in [Transfer history](/web-apps/bridge/transfer-history).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
The route also works in reverse. To move THEO from Robinhood Chain back to Autheo Mainnet (for example, to deposit it on MEXC), set **From** to **Robinhood** and **To** to **Autheo Mainnet**.
</Tip>

<Warning>
The Bridge enforces geographic compliance restrictions. Users in certain jurisdictions may be unable to access the service.
</Warning>
