This page covers claiming your earned rewards, unbinding licenses, and unstaking THEO tokens from the Autheo Validator Portal.

For staking mechanics and reward calculations, see [Staking and rewards](/token/staking-and-rewards).

## Claiming staking rewards

> **Two reward types:** Staking rewards and NFT/license rewards are separate reward streams. This page covers staking rewards. NFT/license rewards are claimed separately through the license reward mechanism.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Delegation page">
    Navigate to the **Delegation** page from the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim from the delegation row">
    Find the delegation you want to claim from and click **Claim Rewards** in the Actions column to claim your staking rewards.

    ![Delegation page with Claim Rewards button highlighted](/images/vp-claim-rewards-1.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in Keplr">
    Sign the transaction in Keplr. Rewards transfer to your wallet upon confirmation.

    ![Claim rewards — Keplr signing prompt](/images/vp-claim-rewards-2.png)

    ![Claim rewards — transaction confirmed](/images/vp-claim-rewards-3.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Unbinding a license

> **Before you unbind:** Unbinding disconnects the license from its current delegation. Review any unclaimed license/NFT rewards before confirming the transaction.

Unbinding releases a Prime or Core license from a delegation. The license enters a cooldown period before it can be rebound to another validator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the delegation">
    On the **Delegation** page, locate the delegation with the license you want to unbind.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the unbind modal">
    Click the **Unbind License** button (shown in red) in the Actions column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the license and review cooldown">
    In the modal, select the license to unbind and review the cooldown period displayed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and sign">
    Click **Unbind License** and sign the transaction in Keplr.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Unbinding a license moves it from `ACTIVE` back to `BOUND` or `ISSUED` status. The license cannot be rebound until the cooldown period expires.
</Warning>

## Unstaking tokens

Unstaking (undelegating) removes your THEO from a validator. Tokens enter an **unbonding period** during which they are locked and do not earn rewards. They become available in your wallet after the unbonding period completes.

> **Unbonding period:** Approximately **21 days**. During this period, unstaked THEO is locked, does not earn staking rewards, and cannot be transferred.

> **Note:** Unstaking and unbinding are different actions. **Unstaking** removes your THEO delegation and starts the token unbonding period. **Unbinding** releases a Prime or Core license from its delegation so the license can later be rebound.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the unstake flow">
    On the **Delegation** page, click the **Unstake** button in the Actions column for the delegation you want to exit.

    ![Delegation page with Unstake button highlighted](/images/vp-unstake-1.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Enter the THEO amount to unstake, or click **MAX** to unstake your full delegated amount.

    ![Unstake amount input modal](/images/vp-unstake-2.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and sign">
    Click **Confirm** and sign the transaction in Keplr.

    ![Unstake transaction confirmed in Keplr](/images/vp-unstake-3.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The unbonding period is an on-chain parameter (~21 days). During this period, your tokens are neither earning rewards nor available for transfer. Plan accordingly before initiating a large unstake.
</Note>

### Moving your delegation to another validator

Autheo does not currently support redelegation.

To move your THEO from one validator to another:

1. Unstake your THEO from the current validator.
2. Wait for the approximately 21-day unbonding period to complete.
3. After the THEO becomes available in your wallet, delegate it to the new validator.

Because the unbonding period is required before redelegation, consider the validator's commission rate and other relevant factors before delegating.

## Selling or trading claimed rewards

Claimed and unstaked THEO lives on Autheo Mainnet. To sell or trade it, bridge it to Base network first, then use a DEX such as [Hydrex](https://www.hydrex.fi/). See [Bridging THEO from Autheo Mainnet to Base](/web-apps/bridge/bridging-theo-to-base) and [Buying and trading THEO on Hydrex](/token/buying-and-trading-theo).
