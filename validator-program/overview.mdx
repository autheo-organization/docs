The Autheo validator program enables **Sovereign license holders** to operate validators and secure the network. **Prime and Core license holders do not operate validator nodes; they delegate THEO to existing validators.**

Each NFT license is a tier-based credential that determines what role you can perform on the network.

## Choose your role

Your license determines how you participate in the Autheo network:

| License | Role | Runs a validator? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sovereign** | Operate a validator and earn validator rewards | Yes |
| **Prime** | Delegate THEO to an existing validator | No |
| **Core** | Delegate THEO to an existing validator | No |

> **Important:** Prime and Core licenses are delegation licenses. They do not require you to run or host a validator node.

## License tiers

| Tier | Role | Annual THEO emission | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sovereign** | Create and operate a validator node | ~187,969 THEO | 1 Sovereign NFT license |
| **Prime** | Delegate to external validators (higher tier) | ~18,797 THEO | 1 Prime NFT license |
| **Core** | Delegate to external validators (standard tier) | ~1,880 THEO | 1 Core NFT license |

Licenses originate as ERC-721 tokens on **Arbitrum** and are bridged to Autheo Chain. A Sovereign license is required to run a validator; Prime and Core licenses enable delegation without running a node.

## Validator responsibilities

Only Sovereign license holders who operate validators have these infrastructure and validator-operations responsibilities. Prime and Core holders participate through delegation and do not operate validator infrastructure.

As a validator, you are responsible for:

- Maintaining a node that meets the [hardware requirements](/nodes/node-setup/node-setup)
- Keeping the node synced and signing blocks with ≥ the minimum liveness threshold
- Securing both the operator key and consensus key
- Monitoring for liveness failures and responding to incidents promptly
- Keeping the binary version up to date

## Reward streams

Validators earn rewards from two independent sources:

1. **Staking rewards** — Proportional to bonded stake; auto-accrued via `x/distribution`; manually withdrawn via `withdraw-all-rewards`
2. **NFT emission rewards** — Fixed per-block allocation based on the Sovereign license tier; manually claimed via `claim-nft-rewards`

See [Staking and rewards](/token/staking-and-rewards) for the full mechanics.

## How to participate

### Sovereign validator operators

If you want to operate a validator:

1. **Acquire a Sovereign NFT license.** Purchase a Sovereign license from the [Node Sale Platform](/web-apps/node-sale/purchasing-a-license) or an approved secondary market.
2. **Set up your infrastructure.** Provision a server that meets the [hardware requirements](/nodes/requirements/hardware) or use a supported managed hosting provider.
3. **Install and configure the node.** Follow the [installation](/nodes/node-setup/installation) and [configuration](/nodes/node-setup/configuration) guides.
4. **Bridge and bind your license.** Follow the [License setup](/nodes/node-setup/license-setup) guide to bridge the license from Arbitrum and bind it on Autheo Chain.
5. **Register your validator.** Submit `MsgCreateValidator` using the [validator registration](/nodes/node-setup/validator-registration) guide.

### Prime and Core delegators

If you want to delegate THEO to an existing validator:

1. **Acquire a Prime or Core NFT license.**
2. **Bridge and bind your license** using the [License setup](/nodes/node-setup/license-setup) guide.
3. **Select an eligible validator** to delegate to.
4. **Delegate your THEO** through the Validator Portal.

Prime and Core holders do not need to provision infrastructure or register a validator.

> **Important:** Autheo does not support redelegation. Once you delegate THEO to a validator, you must unstake and wait approximately 21 days for the tokens to unbond before you can delegate to another validator. Choose your validator carefully.
